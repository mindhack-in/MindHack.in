Frequently Asked Questions

Is Pac-Man free to play on Mindhack.in?

Yes, it's completely free and runs directly in your browser - no download or account needed.

How do I control Pac-Man?

Use your keyboard's arrow keys on desktop, or the on-screen touch controls on mobile, to guide Pac-Man through the maze.

What's the goal of the game?

Eat every dot in the maze while avoiding the four ghosts. Grab a power pellet in a corner and you can turn the tables and eat the ghosts instead, for a short time.
