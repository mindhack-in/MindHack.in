Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Birds matching game free to play?

Yes, this bird-themed memory game is completely free on Mindhack.in, with no download or account required.

How do I play the Birds match the cards game?

Flip two cards at a time and try to find the two that show the same bird. Remembering where each card is as you go is the whole game.

Is this game good for kids and beginners?

Yes - it's a simple, low-pressure recall game that works well for kids and older adults alike, with no time pressure forcing you to rush.
