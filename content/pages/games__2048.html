What is 2048?

2048 is a sliding-tile, number-matching puzzle invented by Gabriele Cirulli in 2014. The core mechanic, combine numbered tiles on a grid by sliding them, so like-values merge (2 + 2 = 4, 4 + 4 = 8…) and your goal is to create the tile value 2048. You can keep going beyond that for higher scores.

When you play the 2048 game online, you're usually given a 4 x 4 grid and the freedom to swipe up/down/left/right to slide tiles. After each move a new tile appears (typically 2 or 4) in a random empty spot. The challenge grows as free spaces shrink and smart planning becomes essential.
