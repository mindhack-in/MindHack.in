What is a sliding puzzle game?

In a sliding puzzle game, you have to move tiles around on a grid to put them in the right order. You can choose from grids that are 3x3, 4x4, or even 10x10, each of which is harder than the last. You can move tiles around in the empty space provided, but you have to plan a few moves ahead each time. It's a test of your memory, logic, patience, and sharpness.
