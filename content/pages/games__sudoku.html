What is Sudoku?

Sudoku is a logic-based number placement puzzle played on a 9x9 grid. The grid is divided into nine 3x3 subgrids (also called blocks or regions).

The objective is simple: fill the empty cells with numbers from 1 to 9 so that each number appears exactly once in every row, column, and 3x3 subgrid.

Although the rule is straightforward, solving a Sudoku puzzle requires logical deduction and strategy. Puzzles can range in difficulty from easy for beginners to extreme for advanced players.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How do you learn to play Sudoku?

To play Sudoku, fill the 9x9 grid so that each row, column, and 3x3 subgrid contains all numbers from 1 to 9 without any duplicates. Start with grids that have more pre-filled numbers to practice.

How long does it take to solve a Sudoku puzzle?

The time to solve a Sudoku puzzle depends on the difficulty level. An easy puzzle can take 5 to 10 minutes, while extreme levels might take 30 minutes or more.
