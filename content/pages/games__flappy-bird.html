Free flappy bird online game

Flappy Bird is a classic arcade-style game where the player has to guide the bird through a series of pipes without touching them or hitting the ground. This is an endless game; the player's score increases as they successfully pass each set of pipes.

There are two types of pipes: one facing upward and the other facing downward. Both pipes are dangerous for the player and colliding with either will result in a game over.

If the player does not take action to keep the bird aloft, it will fall and hit the ground, ending the game immediately.

How to Start the Game on MindHack.in

Once the page loads, click the start button to begin playing.

If the game ends, you can easily restart by clicking the restart button.

How to Play on Desktop

You can play Flappy Bird on desktop browsers. Press the Spacebar or Up Arrow key to flap and fly upwards. Navigate carefully to avoid the pipes.

How to Play on Mobile

On mobile devices, simply tap the screen to flap upwards and keep the bird in the air.

You score one point for every pipe you successfully pass.
