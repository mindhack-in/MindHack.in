About Us

Fast, friction-free browser games built to challenge your mind.

Who We Are

Mindhack.in started as a side project that grew into something bigger. What began as a small personal build by a software engineer has turned into a free, browser-based gaming platform built around one simple idea: brain games shouldn't come with friction.

We're a small, independent operation — no investors, no ad-stuffed pages, no growth-hacking gimmicks. Just a developer who wanted to build something useful and kept adding to it.

Why We Built This

Most “free” games online come with downloads, ads, account signups, or paywalls. Mindhack.in keeps it simple: instant browser-based games with no downloads, no registrations, and no interruptions.

No downloads. No paywalls. No sign-ups. Open your browser, click play, and you're in. Whether you're solving a Sudoku grid on your lunch break or chasing a new personal best on the Rubik's Cube timer, the game loads instantly and stays free — on any device, every time.

What You'll Find Here

Mindhack.in is home to a growing library of brain games and puzzles — Sudoku, 2048, a WCA-style Rubik's Cube timer, Sliding Puzzle, Flappy Bird, and more on the way. Every game is built to be quick to pick up, genuinely challenging, and good for your focus and memory along the way.

We're still early — Mindhack.in has been live for under a year — and we're actively building. New games, better features, and small improvements roll out regularly based on what people actually use and enjoy.

Our Approach

We keep things simple on purpose:

  • Free, always

    Every game on Mindhack.in is free to play, and any new game we add will be too.

  • No download required

    Everything runs directly in your browser — desktop, tablet, or phone.

  • Built to be useful

    Each game is designed with real features (hints, stats, scrambles, difficulty levels) — not just a bare-bones clone.
