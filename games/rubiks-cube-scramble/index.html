What Is a Rubik’s Cube Timer?

A Rubik’s Cube Timer is used to track the solves of the cuber. This is a dedicated stopwatch for cubing. It includes features like:

Spacebar start/stop for realistic timing.

Random scrambles - A unique scramble is given to the user for practice every time.

Average calculations (Ao5, Ao12, Ao100).

+2 second penalty and DNF options.

Dark/light mode.

Permanent data saving with LocalStorage.

CSV export for your solve history.

It’s everything a cuber needs to measure performance, just like in real competitions — but right from your laptop or mobile.
