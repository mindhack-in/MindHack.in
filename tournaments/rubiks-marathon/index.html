Rules of Marathon:-

1. First and most important rule for this competition is you have to signup and complete your details from user profile.

2. You must login, generate new scramble for 3X3 Rubiks cube and solve it. You will be awared 1 point for 1 submission.

3. If you will upload video on Youtube or Instagram, and collab with Mindhack.in you will be awared 5 Points per video

4. You can submit any no of video daily, but its suggested to keep 2 hour break between 2 videos.

5. Only physical cubes are allowed, No virtual cubes, No cube solving apps, No shortcuts

Disqualification Rules

Participant will be eliminated if we detect:-

  1. Fake solves
  2. Wrong scrambles
  3. Submitting same video more than once will give only 1 point
  4. If 2 users submiting same videos,2nd user will be eliminated.

Other Informations

  • Points will be updated daily.
  • It's 1 month competition, so you can start playing any time within the month.
  • Competition will be ends at 31st Dec. 2025, any submission after that will not be counted.
  • If you have submitted a fake solve by mistake, don't worry you can inform us through our social media channels.
  • Its suggested to every user to signup before hand and start solving today, it will help you to becoming comfortable with our plateform.
